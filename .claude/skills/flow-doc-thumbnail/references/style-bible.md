# Style bible: the cinematic documentary thumbnail

A breakdown of the 34 reference thumbnails the user collected (33 unique; `maxresdefault (15).jpg` and `(16).jpg` are the same image). They come from big English documentary / explainer channels on geopolitics, money, tech, crime and science. Use this file to choose the pattern, the look and the title system. Measurements are at 1280×720.

The user keeps the pack in a folder named `Downloads` with the original filenames below. When a concept follows one of them closely, tell the user which file to attach as a **style reference** (see `image-sourcing.md` §4).

## 1. What makes all of them work
1. **One subject, one twist.** Every image has one hero you read in under a second, plus one thing that is "wrong" with it: a pill with a skull, a microphone in a pool of blood, a Nobel medal whose engraved Nobel holds dynamite, an Apple Park ring with one section glowing blue. The twist is the question; the title points at it.
2. **Scale contrast.** A giant face behind a toy-sized bunker, two tiny red figures next to a huge machine, giant hands snapping a plane. Big vs small instantly reads as power, control or danger.
3. **Reveal the hidden.** Cutaways (gas station tank, Air Force One, Venice house piles), torn surfaces (an iPhone ripped open), a magnifier circle on the detail, an X-ray glow. The viewer sees what is normally invisible.
4. **Two or three colors only.** A neutral base (charcoal, white, cream, sky) plus one loud accent: red for danger, cyan/blue for people or holograms, orange/gold for heat, money or the secret detail.
5. **Few words, huge.** 1–4 words, in an empty area made for them, never over the hero's focal point. The text adds what the picture can't say (a number, a verdict, a twist), it never describes the picture.
6. **Photoreal or clean 3D, sharp and high contrast.** No painterly mush, no busy backgrounds, no stock-photo smile.

Rules of thumb:
- Pick the pattern from the episode's **question**: control → P1, mechanism → P2/P3, danger → P4/P5, who did it → P7/P10, money/guilt → P9, place → P11/P13, impossible scale → P8.
- Max 3 elements. If a viewer at phone size can't name the hero in one second, cut.
- The twist must be honest: if it isn't true or at least a fair symbol of the episode, choose another.

## 4. Composition (16:9)
- Hero focal point near a third line; it may be large and bleed off the edges.
- **Title zone**: an empty calm area of ≈ 30–40% of the width or a top band of ≈ 25% of the height, chosen per concept. Default for this channel: the **right ≈ 40%** (Arabic reads right to left), or a **top band** when the hero is wide (cutaways, P13).
- **Bottom-right corner** (x > 82%, y > 85%) always free: YouTube's duration badge sits there.
- Leave a small clear corner for the channel logo (default top-left, ≈ x 3–12%, y 4–18%).
- Faces: eyes sharp, at the top third, looking at the camera or down at the miniature.

## 5. Title system
Four text treatments appear in the pack. Pick one per thumbnail, never mix more than two.

| Style | Look in the pack | Arabic equivalent (free, Google Fonts) | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 Bold block** | heavy geometric sans, ALL CAPS, white with a soft dark shadow, one word in red (`DEADLY`, `KILLED`, `STILL RICH AFTER WW2`) | **Cairo Black** or **Alexandria Black**, white `#FFFFFF`, keyword red `#E3161B`, shadow 0 4px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.6) | P4, P9, P13, dark looks |
| **T2 Label boxes + arrow** | white bold sans in tight black boxes, a thick hand-drawn curved arrow to the twist (`FAKE EMPLOYEE`, `1,000 YEARS OLD`, `NUCLEAR WEAPON`) | **Tajawal ExtraBold** white on black `#111` boxes that hug each line; arrow white (on dark) or black (on light), stroke ≈ 14px, round caps | P2, P7, P13, clean 3D |
| **T3 Editorial serif** | elegant bold newspaper serif, sentence case, one word underlined in red (`We tested it`, `This shouldn't exist.`, `Him.`, `Safe / Lethal`) | **Noto Naskh Arabic Bold** or **Amiri Bold**, white or off-white `#F2EEE6`, a red `#E3161B` underline 6–8px under one word | P4, P5, P6, P8, prestige topics |
| **T4 Big statement on top** | one large line across the top over a textured area, slightly blurred or embossed into the image (`They Got It Wrong`, `The AI Wake-Up Call`) | **Alexandria Bold** or **IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold**, cream `#EDE3D1`, drop shadow | P11, P12 |

Size: the main line is ≈ 12–20% of the image height (85–145 px at 720 high). Short lines; break into 2 lines rather than shrinking.

Copy rules:
- 1–4 words. A verdict, a number, a shocking label or a short question: «قتلوه»، «مميت»، «٣٦ مليار»، «مكانش المفروض يحصل»، «هو.»، «آمن / قاتل».
- Arabic numerals or Hindi-Arabic both fine; pick one per channel and keep it.
- The title adds information the image doesn't show. Image = the twist; text = the stakes.
- No clickbait the episode can't back up.

**Text spec line format to give the user:**
`الخط: Cairo Black أبيض · كلمة «…» بالأحمر #E3161B · الحجم ≈ 16% من ارتفاع الصورة (115px على 1280×720) · مكانه يمين في المساحة الفاضية، من x 60% لـ 95% · ظل أسود ناعم · سيب الركن تحت يمين فاضي عشان مدة الفيديو`

## 6. Reel / Shorts (9:16)
Same concept, look and title style. The hero moves to the lower-middle (focal point y ≈ 45–72%), the title to the top band (y ≈ 14–36%), nothing important in the bottom 20% or on the right edge below y ≈ 55% (app buttons). Vertical patterns work best: P1 (face above, miniature below), P4 (object standing), P6 (hands from the top).
